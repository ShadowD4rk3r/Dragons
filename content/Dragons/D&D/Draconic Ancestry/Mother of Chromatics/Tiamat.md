**Pronunciation*** : TEE-a-mat

Tiamat, she was the goddess of greed, and the queen of evil dragons, while she herself was chaotic evil and lawful evil. Though during a small period, she was reluctantly a servant of [[Bane]] and [[Asmodeus]]. 

She also was apart of the [[Faerûnian]] pantheon, although before she entered, she was a member of the [[Draconic]] and [[Untheric]] as a member of both pantheons.

She was also an eternal rival of her brother [[Bahamut]], who was the ruler of the metallic dragons. These two held an enmity, despite it. Legends say that these two worked together to create the first world, which laid the foundation for Annam to forge the Material Plane.


---
# Description

Tiamat, was a special [[Chromatic dragon]], she had a head for each of the primary color. The color of her head's were the primary colors of the most common species of chromatics; [[Black Dragon]], [[Blue Dragon]], [[Green Dragon]], [[Red Dragon]], and [[White Dragon]]. Each head had the ability to be autonomous alongside retaining the powers of its respective race of dragonkind. It was also seen that she had traits in common with a wyvern, which included but wasn't limited to; a long tail tipped with a venomous stinger.
